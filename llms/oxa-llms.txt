# Oxa

> Oxa (formerly Oxbotica, rebranded in 2023) is a British autonomous vehicle software company founded in 2014 in Oxford, England by Paul Newman and Ingmar Posner. It sells Industrial Mobility Automation — self-driving software for industrial vehicles operating in ports, airports, manufacturing yards, solar farms and shuttle networks — under the banner it calls Universal Autonomy: the ability of any vehicle to operate autonomously in any place at any time.

Oxa's product line has four parts. **Oxa Driver** is the autonomy stack that gives a vehicle full self-driving capability. **Oxa Foundry** (previously marketed as Oxa MetaDriver) is a generative-AI toolchain that virtually replicates and expands real-world driving scenarios to accelerate training and assurance. **Reference Autonomy Designs (RADs)** are modular hardware integration blueprints for retrofitting existing fleets. **Oxa Hub** is the cloud fleet-management suite, covering remote assist, task design, workspace mapping, digital twins, calibration and in-use monitoring for regulatory compliance.

The company raised a $115M Series C in 2023 with participation from Google, and works with Ocado Group, ZF and bp. In 2021 it was the first autonomy company to have its safety case assessed by the BSI, and in 2022 it ran Europe's first zero-occupancy autonomous vehicle journey on public roads.
